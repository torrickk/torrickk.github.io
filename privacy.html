Privacy policy, NZSSV Drive

Last updated 21 November 2026.

NZSSV Drive is used only by the NZSSV team, with the team's own Google account. With the access granted to it, it reads and writes files in that account's Google Drive, only to copy the team's own working files. It collects no personal data, sends data to no one but Google, shows no ads and shares nothing with third parties. Its access token is kept on the team's own computers and cloud workspace. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

NZSSV Drive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
